LAZARUS DEAL RECOVERY
PRODUCT HOOK & RETENTION ENGINE SPECIFICATION
Building Addictive Operational Dependency in Enterprise B2B Sales
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
To transition Lazarus Deal Recovery from an analytical tool into an indispensible enterprise system, the platform implements four core product hook mechanics:

Daily Autonomous Morning Briefings (Push Habit Loop)
Real-Time Gamified Deal Resurrection Scorecards (Dopamine & Rep Competition)
Autonomous Ghost-Buster Mode with Review Safeguards (Zero-Friction Execution)
One-Click Executive & Board-Ready Revenue Leakage Reports (CRO Retention)
1. THE DAILY AUTONOMOUS MORNING BRIEFING (7:30 AM HABIT LOOP)
Mechanic: Pushes actionable, pre-calculated intelligence directly into Slack, Teams, or Email at 7:30 AM local time before the sales rep begins their workday.
Payload Architecture:
Critical Drift Alerts: Highlight deals where DRI jumped > 15 points overnight or a Champion stopped responding.
One-Click Actions: Provide pre-written, grounded pattern interrupts ready for single-click execution or approval.
Zero-Friction CRM Sync: Executed actions update HubSpot/Salesforce automatically.
Goal: Establishes Lazarus as the primary daily ritual for AEs and CROs.
2. REAL-TIME GAMIFIED DEAL RESURRECTION SCORECARD
Mechanic: Converts deal recovery from a tedious task into a visible, competitive team milestone.
System Trigger: Fires automatically when a re-engagement outreach generates a reply or lowers DRI from > 75 to < 30.
Notification Blueprint:
Slack/Teams Channel Broadcast: Real-time alert highlighting rep name, deal value, DRI drop, and rescued revenue.
Rescued Revenue Leaderboard: Tracks monthly resurrected ARR per rep and team.
Goal: Drives rep adoption through public wins and team recognition.
3. AUTONOMOUS GHOST-BUSTER MODE (ZERO-FRICTION EXECUTION)
Mechanic: Operates in the background as an automated revenue assistant for high-risk deals.
Execution Threshold: Triggered when DRI > 85 or unanswered email count >= 3.
Safety Safeguards:
1-Hour Buffer Window: Generates the pattern interrupt email and places it in a review queue.
Auto-Dispatch Rule: If the rep does not edit or cancel within the 1-hour window, the outreach is sent via connected Gmail/Outlook API with complete audit logging.
Goal: Eliminates manual outreach overhead while preventing high-risk deals from falling through the cracks.
4. ONE-CLICK BOARD-READY REVENUE LEAKAGE REPORT (CRO RETENTION)
Mechanic: Equips sales leaders with instantly shareable executive summaries for leadership and board meetings.
Key Metrics Included:
Total Pipeline Risk Leakage: $ Value & DRI Distribution.
Rescued ARR YTD & Win Rate Impact
Root-Cause Bottleneck Breakdown: e.g., Security/Legal vs. Champion Disempowerment.
Goal: Secures long-term enterprise renewal by proving clear ROI directly to executive decision-makers.
5. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION & WEBHOOK CONTRACTS (server/engine/hooks.ts)
Defines the schema structures for Slack/Teams push notifications, autonomous execution queues, and board report generation parameters.
